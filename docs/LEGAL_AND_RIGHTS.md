## 1. Executive Summary & Intent

Dibyt is an open-source, self-hosted file distribution project. The intended license for the new Dibyt product source code, architecture, and documentation is the **Apache License, Version 2.0**.

However, because the project builds upon and preserves historical coursework material originating from an academic group project, strict legal hygiene must be observed. We distinguish between:
1. **Dibyt Core Code & Documentation:** Authored for the Dibyt product; licensed under Apache-2.0.
2. **Historical Academic Coursework:** Preserved under `legacy/academic/cn-project/` for provenance and educational reference; subject to group project attribution and institutional rights boundaries.

## 3. Unresolved Rights & Disclaimers

In compliance with `ANTIGRAVITY_GLOBAL_RULES.md`:
1. **No Blanket Relicensing:** We do not claim that the historical coursework report or co-authored academic files are relicensed under Apache-2.0 without explicit, documented agreement from all named group contributors and review of university coursework policies.
2. **Preservation of Provenance:** Legacy files are retained with original comments and structure in `legacy/academic/cn-project/`.
3. **No Fictitious Entities:** All copyright statements reflect actual authors (Raj Patel for Dibyt product code) and do not invent corporate or organization names.
